# Disclaimer

This software is **not legal advice** and produces no legal conclusions.

- The engine's output is a **derivation** — dated facts, the constraint
  applied, and the arithmetic — never a verdict about a case.
- Given confirmed inputs, the engine is deterministic and exact by
  construction (calendar arithmetic, no model in the loop; enforced in CI).
- **Extraction is assistive and incomplete.** Facts proposed from
  documents must be verified against the quoted source before use.
  Unplaceable and disputed items are surfaced, never resolved silently.
- Where the extractor uses a language model, document text is sent to
  the configured endpoint. For sensitive bundles run fully local
  (Ollama) or use `--from-tdgs`. No telemetry is collected.
